# Goal & Purpose
In Nigerian public institutions (MDAs), critical cyber incidents arrive as chaotic messages: forwarded WhatsApp notes, urgent emails in mixed Nigerian Pidgin and formal English, raw system logs, and frantic phone summaries. Because CSIRTs are understaffed, reports get handled FIFO (First In, First Out), leaving critical ransomware outbreaks or payroll account hijacking waiting behind spam.

KARIYA Sentinel by **Builder OS** serves as Nigeria's autonomous national First Responder:
1. **Urgent-First Priority (SLA Enforcement)**: Instantly score threat severity and escalate P1 Critical threats (e.g. CBN ransomware, IPPIS salary batch modification) with strict 15-minute response SLAs.
2. **Zero-Leakage NDPR / NDPA 2023 Compliance**: Automatically strip sensitive Nigerian personal data (BVN, NIN, 10-digit NUBAN accounts, phone numbers, officer names) before sharing outward.
3. **Massive Deduplication**: Group repeat reports from multiple staff and agencies into unified incident campaigns (95.8% alert fatigue reduction).
4. **Resilience to Power & Network Outages (Rule 06)**: Operate 100% locally with zero external cloud dependencies and SQLite WAL atomic persistence during national grid collapse or subsea fiber cuts.
5. **Statutory Routing & Forensic Rigor**: Extract actionable IOCs (IPs, domains, hashes, CVEs) and route immediately to statutory bodies (**ngCERT**, **NITDA**, **EFCC/NFIU**, **IPPIS/OAGF**).

# Operational Philosophy
- **High Data Density & Human Craft**: No generic AI fluff, no cartoonish gradients. Designed like tier-1 security platforms (Sentry, Linear, CrowdStrike).
- **Explainable AI (Rule 05)**: Transparent machine learning (TF-IDF + Logistic Regression) with documented confusion matrix, per-class F1-scores, and honest boundary analysis.
- **Zero Spend & Privacy Safe (Rules 01 & 04)**: Free, open-source stack with synthetic realistic ground-truth dataset.
